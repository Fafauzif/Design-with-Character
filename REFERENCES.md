## Visual references

- Lemomo's [Lemo-Opuscar](https://lemomo-ai.github.io/lemo-opuscar/) informed the idea of a selectable creative vocabulary. [Halftone Dossier](https://lemomo-ai.github.io/lemo-opuscar/styles/halftone-dossier/) and [16-bit Pixel RPG](https://lemomo-ai.github.io/lemo-opuscar/styles/pixel-rpg/) informed print treatment and pixel staging. We authored the demo code, prompts, descriptions and subjects for these web experiences. Upstream applies different licenses to source, prompts, and rendered materials. [Upstream license map](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/LICENSE).
- [Unlikely Collaborators](https://www.unlikelycollaborators.com/), [Reasons to Stay](https://reasonstostay.org/) and the accepted [TEDxUI Letters to Grow project](https://github.com/Fafauzif/tedxui-testing) informed the object-led entrance and simple reading path. [MotionSites](https://motionsites.ai/) and [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/) are discovery references.
- Review criteria draw on the W3C explanations of [text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html), [visible focus](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible.html), and [animation from interaction](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html). Checks in this kit cover selected behaviors and route samples.

When contributing, credit the actual file you used. Include a source URL, creator, license, and modifications when the source terms require them. A link to an inspiration site does not license the assets on that site.
