Sunlight

Folding sundial

Open the lid and the small blade gives the sun somewhere to put a shadow.

The blade stands proud of the dial. The two halves close like a pocket watch.

From the keeper’s log

A pencilled crescent on the step marked the case’s usual place. By late autumn the kitchen chimney shaded the mark, so the keeper carried the whole experiment three steps into the garden.
