Dew

Dew mirror

Where a pane would fog, the mirror shows individual beads. Along the cold upper edge they sit like a row of pinheads.

A shallow tilt keeps a newly formed drop in view before it rolls to the rim.

From the keeper’s log

On a still morning the keeper found the face dotted and its wooden foot dry. The sketch from that day gives each drop a circle of its own. By the time the kettle boiled, there were fewer circles to draw.
