Frost

Frost comb

Seven scraps of steel give frost seven different edges to catch. The deepest cut can hold white crystals after the tips have cleared.

Each tine has its own notch. The dark wood makes a pale deposit easier to see.

From the keeper’s log

The keeper marked the wooden foot with an arrow so the comb could face the orchard every night. One November sketch records a feather of ice bridging two teeth before the sun reached the ledge.
