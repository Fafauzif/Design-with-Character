# Style families

Choose by what a person will do. If a six-page brief requests a named family, preserve its strongest operational idea across the experience while changing how each page works. Read a source kit's style catalog, individual STYLE.md and working examples when available. A newly registered style in that project joins the options below; this starter list is open.

| Family | Signature in action | Transfer and restraint |
|---|---|---|
| Halftone Dossier | Read an illustrated case file, examine observations, and stamp a conclusion. | Isolate halftone ink around the subject. Keep notes readable at phone size and use the stamp for an actual decision. A cat portrait doesn't make every pet app a dossier. |
| Botanical Letterpress | Pull a tab or open a folio to reveal someone's letter or a pressed specimen. | Give the fold functional meaning and preserve the voice of the letter. Use the color of the garden or campaign at the entrance, with a quieter surface where someone reads. Skip ruled mastheads and publication furniture unless the content needs them. |
| Specimen Cabinet | Find an object through a cabinet, examine it, compare or collect a note. | Put detail into the specimens and the opening action. A serious comparison or field notebook needs its own composition and direct route. |
| Arcade Workshop | Play or adjust a tangible instrument and immediately see or hear a result. | Arrange the layout around the actual signal path, sequence, or controls. Reuse the meaningful rhythm, not a dashboard covered in glowing square buttons. |
| Pixel Study Room | Start a session with a companion and see the room respond as time passes. | Use crisp art and a readable, live timer. Keep essential controls accessible outside dense scenery. New art must respect any explicitly approved existing character or version. |

When these families all feel wrong, identify what their mechanics omit. For a public dance score, the visitors' shared timing could carry the composition without a physical cabinet, folded letter or hardware console. Describe the proposed action at rest, in use and on return. A new style can be quiet, typographic, photographic, spatial, or behavioral. A convincing original website need not look handmade.

For visual study, inspect [Lemo-Opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), [Unlikely Collaborators](https://www.unlikelycollaborators.com/), [Reasons to Stay](https://reasonstostay.org/), [MotionSites](https://motionsites.ai/) and [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/) as relevant. Name one borrowed principle and one deliberate departure. Distinguish inspiration from assets redistributed under a license.
