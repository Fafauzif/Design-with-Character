# Specimen Cabinet

## Where it fits

Use this style for a small collection visitors can explore one object at a time. It works for museum holdings, materials samples, or a maker’s archive. The central object is the cabinet, with heavy fitted drawers and visible pulls. The welcome text shares the page with the cabinet and points to a drawer.

## Compose the cabinet

- Make each drawer a named entry with a large target and a recognizable pull.
- Reveal the object, name, and a short observation together in the drawer. Keep its notes close to the image.
- Set cabinet and paper apart through material and color. This demo uses dark wine enamel, brass pulls, ivory paper, and a vermilion action.
- Use spacing and scale for hierarchy. Reserve lines and bevels for the furniture.

## Interaction

Use native disclosure controls for opening and closing. Keep the disclosed content next in the keyboard order. The tray enters with a short translation, and reduced motion shows the result immediately. Introduce extra actions only when they support the collection. This demo lets visitors keep an object in field notes and reopen it by name.

## Images and content

The instrument names and descriptions are original fictional exhibit copy. The object photographs are generated with GPT Image as original cutouts for this demo. Hardware, cabinet, and motion are drawn in CSS.
