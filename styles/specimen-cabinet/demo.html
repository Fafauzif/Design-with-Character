The Weather Cabinet

Open a drawer to see how one of three imagined instruments reads the weather.

Field notes (0)

Keep an instrument from a drawer to find it here.

    01Rain

    Rain catcher

    Set the brass foot level before the first drops arrive. When the rain passes, read the waterline at eye level and empty the glass.

    View field notes
    02Wind

    Pocket wind counter

    Hold the cups above your shoulder and count the turns for half a minute. Repeating the count from a sheltered spot shows how much a wall slows the air.

    View field notes
    03Sun

    Folding sundial

    Raise the little brass blade and turn the case to face true north. Its shadow traces local solar time along the engraved ring.
