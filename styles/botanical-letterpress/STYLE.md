# Botanical Letterpress

Build the opening around a physical paper object that holds the content. In this demo, a pressed ivory cosmos stretches over a cream folio. Opening it brings the specimen aside and folds a field note forward. A blue binding tab connects the printed object to the only action. Give the type room to breathe and let the specimen overlap the paper edge.

Use moss as the ambient color, cream where a note is held and ultramarine where the visitor acts. A display serif can make one title feel printed. Keep the action label and body copy clear at normal reading sizes and align the composition asymmetrically. On a phone, stack the copy and artwork and keep the opener within reach. Make all content and focus transitions work with reduced motion.

The composition takes its folded-paper interaction from the [TEDxUI Letters to Grow reference](https://github.com/Fafauzif/tedxui-testing). The cosmos is an original botanical cutout made with GPT Image. The fictional note and HTML/CSS are original to this demo.

## Across a larger site

Carry the fold into the entrance to a letter collection. A reader arriving directly at a letter should be able to read it immediately and move to a neighboring letter. Keep the cobalt tab as the cue for moving through the collection. A submission form can use the same ink and paper while leaving the specimen outside the writing area. At larger scale, reserve full-size botanical art for selected entries whose contents give the species a reason to appear.
